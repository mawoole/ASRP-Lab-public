# ASRP-Lab — Public Roadmap

This roadmap describes the intended direction of the project. It is a planning document, not a contractual commitment.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- define research-program entities;
- structure projects, objectives, milestones, teams, and organizations;
- establish user and role management;
- create the initial application architecture;
- implement backend and frontend foundations;
- establish auditability and traceability principles.

## Phase 2 — Research Program Management

- project and program dashboards;
- milestone tracking;
- research team coordination;
- budget and resource monitoring;
- document and evidence management;
- progress reporting;
- risk and dependency tracking.

## Phase 3 — Evaluation and Coordination

- structured project evaluation;
- duplication / overlap detection;
- relationship mapping between research programs;
- outcome and impact indicators;
- cross-program reporting;
- decision-support dashboards.

## Phase 4 — Advanced Analytics and AI Assistance

Potential areas:

- semantic search;
- assisted synthesis;
- similarity and overlap analysis;
- research portfolio analysis;
- support for identifying gaps and opportunities.

AI functions must include confidentiality, data-protection, traceability, and human-review controls.

## Phase 5 — Public / Institutional Deployment

- security review;
- privacy and data-protection review;
- performance and resilience validation;
- operational monitoring;
- deployment documentation;
- pilot program.

## Principle

ASRP-Lab is intended to improve the organization of research **without replacing scientific judgment**.
